About Yellowfir

Yellowfir Group Limited 是一家注册在香港的综合性可再生能源服务公司（公司注册号：78751009），专注于新能源开发、投资及相关领域的整合服务。董事团队曾任职于中国内地大型国有电力央企核心战略投资岗位，拥有超过十年风电、光伏电站开发、投资、建设及运营全链条实战经验。

 

公司以“绿色能源，点亮未来”为使命，通过技术创新与国际合作，为目标国家提供高效可持续的能源方案。目前核心布局中亚，深耕吉尔吉斯斯坦市场，与本地伙伴联合开发600MW光伏项目，并规划2GW绿色能源+绿色农牧产业园，年均可提供30亿度清洁电力，成为中亚绿色经济标杆。

 

核心竞争力：本地化运营（深度理解政策法规）、国际化合作（整合全球领先技术及资本）、专业顾问团队（涵盖投资架构、财税法律、项目全周期管理）。Yellowfir 致力于成为跨境可再生能源与综合咨询领域的引领者。

 

Yellowfir Group Limited is a Hong Kong-registered comprehensive renewable energy service company (Company Registration No.: 78751009), specializing in new energy development, investment, and integrated services across related sectors. Its board members have previously held core strategic investment roles in major state-owned power enterprises in mainland China, bringing over a decade of hands-on experience across the entire value chain of wind and solar power development, investment, construction, and operation.

 

Driven by the mission of "Green Energy, Powering a Bright Future," the company delivers efficient and sustainable energy solutions to target countries through technological innovation and international cooperation. With a strategic focus on Central Asia, Yellowfir has established a strong presence in the Kyrgyzstan market, co-developing a 600MW solar PV project in partnership with local stakeholders. The company is also planning a 2GW green energy + green agriculture and animal husbandry industrial park, capable of generating 3 billion kWh of clean electricity annually, positioning itself as a benchmark for green economy development in Central Asia.

 

Core Competitiveness: Localized operations (deep understanding of policies and regulations), international collaboration (integrating world-class technologies and capital), and expert advisory teams (covering investment structuring, tax, legal, and full project lifecycle management). Yellowfir is committed to becoming a leader in cross-border renewable energy and comprehensive consulting services.
